About GreenPulse Community

Trees are natural climate protectors. Through GreenPulse, students and communities can build a transparent, verifiable plantation culture where every planted sapling is recorded and celebrated.

Why Participate?

  • Improve local air quality and reduce heat islands.
  • Create a visual social proof of environmental action.
  • Inspire peers to adopt sustainable daily habits.

Together, we grow cleaner cities and stronger communities.
